# Delivery Schedule and Cut-off Times

Each city is served from its own warehouse. Orders are delivered from the warehouse in the
outlet's city only: Dubai from **DXB**, Abu Dhabi from **AUH**, Sharjah from **SHJ**.

## Cut-off times

| City | Warehouse | Order cut-off for next-day delivery | Delivery days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dubai | DXB | 4:00 PM | Monday – Saturday |
| Sharjah | SHJ | 3:00 PM | Monday – Saturday |
| Abu Dhabi | AUH | 2:00 PM | Sunday – Friday |

Orders confirmed after the cut-off are delivered the day after next. Orders waiting for credit
approval (PENDING_APPROVAL) are scheduled only once a manager approves them, using the time of
approval against the cut-off.

## Delivery windows

- GROCERY and MODERN_TRADE: 7:00 AM – 1:00 PM.
- HORECA: 6:00 AM – 11:00 AM, so hotels and restaurants receive stock before lunch service.
- Specific time requests (for example "deliver before 10am") go in the order notes. They are
  best effort and not guaranteed.

## Minimum drop size

The minimum delivery is **10 cases** per order (any mix of products). Smaller orders are added to
the outlet's next scheduled delivery instead of a separate trip.

## Public holidays and Ramadan

There are no deliveries on Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha public holidays. During Ramadan, delivery
windows move to 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM and all cut-off times move one hour earlier.

## Missed deliveries

If the outlet is closed or refuses the delivery without a valid reason (damage or wrong items),
the order is returned to the warehouse and re-delivered on the next delivery day.
The rep is notified to follow up with the outlet.
