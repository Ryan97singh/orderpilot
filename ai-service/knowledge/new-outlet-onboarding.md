# New Outlet Onboarding

How a sales rep opens an account for a new outlet, from first visit to first order.

## Who can be onboarded

Licensed trade customers in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah in one of three channels: GROCERY
(groceries, baqalas, mini-marts), HORECA (hotels, restaurants, cafes) and MODERN_TRADE
(supermarket and hypermarket chains, handled by key account managers).

## Documents required

- Valid trade licence (copy), with the activity covering food and beverage sale or service.
- Emirates ID or passport copy of the owner or authorised signatory.
- VAT registration certificate (TRN), if registered.
- Signed customer application form with delivery address, contact person and opening hours.
- For credit accounts: a security cheque for the requested credit limit.

## Steps

1. The rep visits the outlet, collects the documents and photos of the shopfront and storage area.
2. The rep submits the application; the outlet is assigned to the rep as sales rep.
3. Credit control checks the documents and sets the channel, credit limit and payment terms,
   usually within **2 working days**.
4. The outlet gets an outlet code (format `OUT-1234`) and appears in OrderPilot.
5. The first order can be placed immediately after approval.

## First orders

Until the credit limit is approved, the first order can be placed as cash on delivery (COD).
New outlets start with the lower end of their channel's credit range (see the credit policy) and
can request a review after three months of on-time payments.

## Warehouse assignment

The outlet's city decides its warehouse: Dubai outlets are served from DXB, Abu Dhabi from AUH and
Sharjah from SHJ. An outlet that moves to another city must be re-registered with the new address.

## Welcome pack

New GROCERY outlets can receive a starter promotion approved by the area sales manager, for
example a discount on their first FizzUp and Pure Peak Water orders, created as a regular
promotion in the system.
