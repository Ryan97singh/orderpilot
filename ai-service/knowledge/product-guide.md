# Product Guide

Our range covers four brands. All products are sold **by the case**, and prices are per case in
AED excluding VAT. Live prices, promotions and stock are always in the OrderPilot catalogue; this
guide explains the range and how to talk about it.

## SKU format

SKUs follow `BRAND-VARIANT-SIZE-UNITSPERCASE`, for example `FZ-COLA-330-24` is FizzUp Cola,
330ml, 24 units per case. Brand prefixes: FZ (FizzUp), OJ (Oasis Juice), PP (Pure Peak Water),
VX (VoltX Energy).

## FizzUp (carbonated soft drinks)

The core brand for grocery and HORECA. Flavours: Cola, Cola Zero, Lemon-Lime and Orange.

- 330ml cans, 24 per case: the best seller for groceries and cafeterias.
- 1.5L PET bottles, 6 per case: family packs for groceries and supermarkets.
- Shelf life: 9 months for cans, 6 months for PET.
- Selling tip: Cola Zero is growing fast in modern trade; place it next to regular Cola.

## Oasis Juice (juices)

Chilled-aisle juices with no added sugar. Flavours: Orange, Mango and Apple.

- 1L cartons, 12 per case.
- 200ml cartons, 24 per case: popular with schools and HORECA breakfast service.
- Shelf life: 6 months unopened. Store below 25°C; keep out of direct sun.

## Pure Peak Water (bottled water)

High-volume, low-margin. Drives delivery drop size, so suggest it with every order.

- 500ml bottles, 24 per case.
- 1.5L bottles, 6 per case.
- Shelf life: 12 months.

## VoltX Energy (energy drinks)

Premium energy drinks for petrol-station shops, groceries and modern trade. Original and Sugar-Free.

- 250ml cans, 24 per case.
- Shelf life: 12 months.
- Not for sale to schools; do not promote to minors.

## Stock and warehouses

Stock is held in three warehouses: **DXB** (Dubai), **AUH** (Abu Dhabi) and **SHJ** (Sharjah).
An order is fulfilled only from the warehouse of the outlet's city: Dubai outlets from DXB,
Abu Dhabi outlets from AUH, Sharjah outlets from SHJ. If that warehouse is short, the quote
shows a stock warning and the line cannot be ordered until the quantity is reduced.
