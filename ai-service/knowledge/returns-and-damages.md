# Returns and Damages Policy

This policy covers damaged, leaking, short-delivered and expired stock. Credit notes are issued in
AED against the outlet's account.

## Damaged or leaking stock at delivery

The outlet must check cases in front of the driver. Damaged, leaking or crushed cases are refused
on the spot and noted on the delivery note signed by both sides. No return request is needed:
the invoice is reduced automatically.

## Damage found after delivery

Concealed damage (found when a sealed case is opened) must be reported within **48 hours** of
delivery. The rep logs a return with photos of the product and the batch code. Credit control
issues a credit note within 5 working days after the goods are collected.

## Short delivery

If fewer cases arrive than invoiced, the outlet must note the shortage on the delivery note.
Shortages reported later than 24 hours after delivery cannot be accepted.

## Expired and near-expiry stock

- Products returned with **more than 30 days** of shelf life left are not accepted as expiry
  returns; the outlet should rotate stock (first expiry, first out).
- Expired product is credited at **50%** of the invoiced price for GROCERY outlets that followed
  the agreed order quantities, up to 2% of their monthly purchases.
- MODERN_TRADE expiry terms follow each chain's trading agreement.
- HORECA outlets are not eligible for expiry credit.

## Products that cannot be returned

Products sold under a clearance promotion, and products damaged by the outlet's storage
(for example, cans left in direct sun), cannot be returned.

## How to log a return

Returns are logged by the rep during the visit with: outlet code, SKU, number of cases, batch
code, reason, and photos. The warehouse that delivered the order (DXB, AUH or SHJ) collects
returns on the next scheduled delivery to that outlet.
